Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026 · applies to the DeadlineSync PK website and the DeadlinePinPK Chrome extension
Short version: your deadlines stay on your device. We have no accounts, no server that stores your messages, no ads and no tracking of what you write.

What stays on your device

  • The deadlines you save, your settings and any backup you download are stored in your browser (localStorage / extension storage) on your own phone or computer.
  • Messages you paste or share into the app are read by code running in your browser. They are not sent to us.
  • The WhatsApp Web helper in the Chrome extension only reads a message when you click its pin button.

When data leaves your device (only if you choose)

  • Add to calendar opens Google Calendar with the event filled in, or downloads an .ics file. Google then handles it under its own policy.
  • Connect Google Calendar (optional): you sign in with Google. The app asks only for permission to create and manage its own "DeadlinePK" calendar (scope calendar.app.created); it cannot read your other calendars. The sign-in token stays in your browser. Disconnect any time in More tools.
  • Anonymous usage counts (only if the site owner switched it on): the app can send a few anonymous event names (for example "deadline saved", "calendar added", "page opened") with a random ID kept on your device, to the owner's private PostHog project. It never sends your messages, titles, dates, files or IP address, sets no cookies, and does no session recording. It is off when your browser sends Do Not Track, or if you open the app once with ?analytics=off added to the address.
  • AI key (optional): if you paste your own Claude API key, text you ask it to read (a PDF, photo or messages) is sent to Anthropic using your key. Without a key, nothing is sent.
  • Sharing: the WhatsApp buttons create a message and link; you decide who receives it. A shared deadline link contains the title and date inside the link itself.
  • Reporting a wrong date: the "Wrong date? Tell us" link opens a GitHub issue form pre-filled with the message. Nothing is sent until you press submit. Remove names first if you like.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no selling data.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies on this site.
  • No access to your contacts, location or WhatsApp account.

Your control

Clear your browser's site data (or uninstall the extension) to delete everything. Use More tools → Download backup before you do if you want to keep your list.

Hosting and source

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log ordinary web-server data such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. The code is open source (MIT): github.com/Gi-v/deadline-pk.
